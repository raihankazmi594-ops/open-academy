Start with the classics

Plato — Apology

Socrates explains why a life of questioning matters. A strong first philosophical text.

Read free at Project Gutenberg ↗

Epictetus — Enchiridion

A short introduction to Stoicism and the distinction between what is and isn't in our control.

Read free at MIT Classics ↗

Bertrand Russell — The Problems of Philosophy

A clear introduction to knowledge, appearance, reality and philosophical doubt.

Read free at Project Gutenberg ↗

John Stuart Mill — Utilitarianism

A classic argument that actions should be judged partly by their consequences for happiness.
